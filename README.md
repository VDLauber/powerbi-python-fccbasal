What is Harris-Benedict BMR formula?

The Harris-Benedict formula is a mathematical equation used to estimate a person’s Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) — the number of calories the body needs to maintain basic functions at rest, such as breathing, circulation, and cell production.

The formula calculates BMR based on four main factors: sex, age, weight, and height.

For men:
BMR = 88.36 + (13.4 × weight in kg) + (4.8 × height in cm) − (5.7 × age in years)

For women:
BMR = 447.6 + (9.2 × weight in kg) + (3.1 × height in cm) − (4.3 × age in years)

After calculating BMR, it is usually multiplied by an activity factor to estimate Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE), which represents the total calories needed per day based on activity level.

In short, the Harris-Benedict equation helps estimate how many calories a person burns at rest and is widely used for diet planning and fitness programs.
